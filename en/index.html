Why it stays smooth

Three complaints come up again and again with tools like this: the picture lags, it drops out, and a weak network turns it into a slideshow. We handled all three.

Frames don't queue

Many tools buffer frames before sending them, in the name of stability. The cost is that you always see the past. Fovea does the opposite — the newest frame goes straight through, late ones are dropped.

Weak network, still smooth

When the link can't keep up, quality drops a little to protect smoothness, then climbs back once it recovers. You don't have to guess at settings.

Reconnects itself

A loose cable or a WiFi hiccup won't end your stream. The picture comes back on its own.

Three steps

No IP addresses, no commands to type.

1

Install the plugin

Download the file for your system, drop the folder into the OBS plugin location, restart OBS.

2

Open the app

Plug in USB, or make sure the phone and computer share a WiFi network. Press start.

3

Add the source

Sources → Add → Fovea. The picture appears, and from there it behaves like any camera.
